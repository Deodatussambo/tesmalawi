CUSTOMISE BEFORE USING FOR A CLIENT
- Replace YOUR COMPANY, AC logo placeholder, phone, email and location.
- Replace all sample product names, prices, photos and descriptions with verified client information.
- Replace WhatsApp number in the link https://wa.me/265000000000.
- Update brand colours in :root in assets/css/style.css.
- Confirm product categories, stock and prices with the client.
- The contact form is a front-end demo only; connect it to an email service or backend.
- The cart is a visual demonstration only; it does not place real orders.
- No real payment gateway, customer accounts or order database is included.
- Use licensed product images or photographs supplied by the client.

This is a design/reference starter, not a production-ready e-commerce platform.
